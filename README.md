# SAFFLEX-FOR-ALL

Making digital payments and online communication safer for everyone.

SAFFLEX is an Android application designed to help everyday users identify suspicious and potentially dangerous links.


Our Mission

To make online safety accessible to shopkeepers, small businesses, and non-technical users, especially in Tier-2, Tier-3, suburban and rural India.

✨ Planned Features

Automatic link scanning when a link is shared with SAFFLEX

Offline scam detection using local security rules.

Simple risk warnings: Low Risk, Suspicious, and High Risk.

English, Hindi, Marathi and Bengali voice alerts.

Scan history.

Simple and accessible interface.


Risk score from 0–100 with clear risk categories.

Detailed explanations of suspicious link indicators.

Personalized safety recommendations explaining what users should do next.

Multilingual voice warnings and safety instructions.

Our Innovation

SAFFLEX aims to make link safety checks simple, accessible and understandable without requiring users to have technical knowledge.
